# Siapp Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 22 August 2026
**Last updated:** 22 August 2026

Siapp ("**Siapp**", "**we**", "**us**", "**our**") provides a project-tracking
and client-notification platform for construction and legal firms in Malaysia (the
"**Service**"). This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, how we use
it, and the rights available to you under the Malaysian **Personal Data Protection Act
2010 ("PDPA")**.

## 1. Who is responsible for your data

- **Firms (our customers)** who use Siapp to manage projects are the **data
  controllers** of their own clients' and collaborators' personal data. Siapp acts
  as a **data processor** on their behalf.
- **Siapp** is the **data controller** for account, billing, and platform-usage data
  of the firms and firm users who register with us directly.

If you are a client or collaborator of a firm and want to access, correct, or delete
your personal data, please contact **that firm** — they control your data. We will
assist the firm as their processor.

## 2. Data we collect

| Category | Examples | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Account data | Name, work email, password hash, role, workspace | Firm user at sign-up |
| Firm/billing data | Firm name, business address, plan tier, invoices | Firm; billing provider |
| Recipient contact data | Client/collaborator name, **mobile number**, email, language preference | Entered by the firm |
| Messaging data | Message content, delivery/read receipts, opt-in and opt-out records, timestamps | Generated by the Service; Twilio; Meta (WhatsApp) |
| Project content | Projects, tasks, notes, uploaded documents | Firm users; clients (portal uploads) |
| Usage & device data | IP address, browser type, log and diagnostic data | Automatically collected |

## 3. How we use your data (lawful basis)

- **Provide the Service** — deliver project updates, portal access, and notifications
  (performance of contract).
- **Send notifications** — outbound WhatsApp and SMS project updates on behalf of the
  firm, based on **opt-in consent** captured by the firm (see Section 5).
- **Billing and account management** — invoicing and support (contract / legitimate
  interest).
- **Security, fraud prevention, and service improvement** (legitimate interest).
- **Legal compliance** — retention, breach notification, lawful requests.

## 4. Mobile information and message-program privacy (Twilio / SMS)

- **No sharing or selling of mobile numbers.** **We do not share, sell, rent, or
  otherwise disclose mobile phone numbers, or the consent to receive messages, to any
  third party or affiliate for their own marketing or promotional purposes.** Mobile
  numbers are used solely to deliver the messages you have opted in to receive, and
  are shared only with the sub-processors strictly necessary to deliver those messages
  (e.g. our telecommunications provider, Twilio; and, for WhatsApp, Meta). No mobile
  information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional
  purposes.
- **Message frequency.** Messages are transactional and event-driven. You will receive
  messages when project events occur (for example: project welcome, task assignment,
  status change, due-date reminders). **Message frequency varies** by the activity on
  your project and typically ranges from a few messages per week to occasional
  messages, depending on how active your project is.
- **Message and data rates.** **Message and data rates may apply**, depending on your
  mobile carrier and plan.
- **Opt-out.** You can opt out at any time by replying **STOP** to any message. You may
  reply **HELP** for assistance, or contact support@siapp.app. See our
  [SMS / Messaging Terms](./sms-messaging-terms.md) and
  [Campaign Privacy Policy](./campaign-privacy-policy.md) for full program terms.

## 5. Consent, opt-in, and opt-out

- The firm captures your **opt-in consent** to receive WhatsApp/SMS notifications and
  records the timestamp, source, language, and consent copy.
- **All outbound messaging is opt-in.** Recipients who have not consented are
  suppressed and receive no messages.
- You can withdraw consent at any time by replying **STOP**. We process opt-outs across
  all senders and honor them going forward.

## 6. Who we share data with (sub-processors)

We share personal data only with vetted sub-processors under a Data Processing
Addendum, strictly to operate the Service:

| Sub-processor | Purpose | Data |
|---|---|---|
| Twilio | SMS / WhatsApp message delivery | Mobile number, message content, delivery status |
| Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform) | WhatsApp message delivery | Mobile number, message content |
| Google Cloud / Firebase | Application hosting and storage | All Service data |
| Payment provider | Billing | Firm billing data |
| Error and analytics provider | Reliability and diagnostics | Usage/device data |

We **do not** sell personal data. We do not use mobile numbers for third-party
marketing.

## 7. Cross-border transfer

The Service is hosted in Singapore (ap-southeast-1). Where personal data is transferred
outside Malaysia, we ensure a comparable level of protection through contractual
safeguards with the recipient, consistent with the PDPA.

## 8. Retention

We retain personal data for as long as the firm's account is active and as required to
provide the Service, then delete or anonymize it in line with the firm's instructions
and our legal obligations. Opt-out records are retained as needed to honor your choice.

## 9. Your rights under the PDPA

Subject to the PDPA, you may request to **access**, **correct**, **withdraw consent**,
or request **deletion** of your personal data. If you are a client/collaborator, direct
these requests to the **firm** (the data controller). For data we control directly,
contact us at support@siapp.app. We aim to respond within 21 days.

## 10. Security

We apply role-based access control, encryption in transit, and access logging. In the
event of a personal data breach, we follow our breach-response runbook and notify as
required by law.

## 11. Children

The Service is intended for business users and is not directed at children under 18.

## 12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be notified via the Service or email,
and the "Last updated" date will change.
