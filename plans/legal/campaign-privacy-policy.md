# Siapp Messaging Campaign Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 22 August 2026
**Last updated:** 22 August 2026

This Campaign Privacy Policy describes how **Siapp** ("**Siapp**", "**we**")
handles information collected through its SMS/WhatsApp messaging program sent from its
Twilio-provisioned phone number(s), on behalf of the construction and legal firms that
use Siapp. It supplements our full [Privacy Policy](./privacy-policy.md).

## 1. What the program does

Siapp sends **outbound, transactional project notifications** (for example: project
welcome, task assignments, status changes, and due-date reminders) to clients and
collaborators of firms that use the Service. Messages are triggered by project events;
we do not send marketing or promotional messages through this program.

## 2. Consent and opt-in

- Recipients are added by the firm managing their project. The firm captures and records
  **opt-in consent** (timestamp, source, language, and consent copy) before any message
  is sent.
- Only recipients who have opted in receive messages; non-consented recipients are
  suppressed.

## 3. Mobile information — non-sharing statement

**We do not share, sell, rent, or otherwise disclose mobile phone numbers, opt-in
consent, or SMS/messaging consent to any third party or affiliate for their own
marketing or promotional purposes.** Mobile numbers and message content are shared only
with the service providers strictly necessary to deliver the messages you have opted in
to receive:

- **Twilio** — telecommunications provider that transmits SMS/WhatsApp messages;
- **Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform)** — for WhatsApp delivery.

**No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or
promotional purposes.**

## 4. Message frequency

Messages are event-driven and **frequency varies** with the activity on your project.
You may receive anywhere from a few messages per week to occasional messages, depending
on how active your project is.

## 5. Message and data rates

**Message and data rates may apply**, depending on your mobile carrier and plan. Siapp
does not charge recipients for messages.

## 6. Opt-out and help

- Reply **STOP** to any message to unsubscribe. After you send STOP, you will receive a
  confirmation and no further messages, unless you opt in again.
- Reply **HELP** for assistance, or contact us at support@siapp.app / +1 (206) 596-7128.
- We process opt-outs across all senders and honor them going forward.

Full program terms are in our [SMS / Messaging Terms](./sms-messaging-terms.md).

## 7. Data we collect in this program

Mobile number, message content, delivery and read receipts, opt-in and opt-out records,
and related timestamps — used solely to deliver and manage the messaging program.

## 8. Retention

We retain messaging records and opt-out records only as long as needed to operate the
program, honor opt-outs, and meet legal obligations.

## 9. Your rights (PDPA)

If you are a client/collaborator, direct access, correction, or deletion requests to the
**firm** managing your project (the data controller). For data Siapp controls directly,
contact support@siapp.app.
