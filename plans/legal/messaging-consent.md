# Siapp Messaging Consent & Opt-In

**Last updated:** 23 August 2026

This page explains how end-users consent to receive SMS and WhatsApp messages through
Siapp, and shows the exact opt-in disclosure (Call to Action) presented to them. It
exists so that recipients, firms, and messaging carriers can verify how consent is
obtained. It supplements our [Privacy Policy](./privacy-policy.md),
[SMS / Messaging Terms](./sms-messaging-terms.md), and
[Campaign Privacy Policy](./campaign-privacy-policy.md).

## Who receives messages

Siapp is a project-tracking platform used by construction and legal firms in Malaysia.
The firms use Siapp to send **project-status updates** to **their own clients and
collaborators** — the people already engaged on a project with that firm. Siapp does
not message the general public and does not sell or share mobile numbers.

## What messages you will receive

Messages are **transactional project notifications** only — for example: a project
welcome, task assignments, status changes, and due-date reminders. We do not send
marketing or promotional messages.

## How you opt in (Call to Action)

Your consent is collected by the firm **before any message is sent**, through one of two
paths:

1. **Written intake / engagement form.** When you engage the firm, you provide your
   mobile number and check a consent box — **unchecked by default** — on the firm's
   client intake or engagement form, agreeing to receive recurring project-update
   messages from the firm via Siapp.
2. **Verbal agreement during onboarding.** You confirm your agreement verbally, and the
   firm records your opt-in in Siapp with a timestamp, source, and language.

Consent is **not** a condition of any purchase. At the point of opt-in you are shown the
following disclosure:

> **☐ I agree to receive project update messages from [Firm Name] via Siapp by SMS
> and/or WhatsApp.** Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.
> Reply STOP to unsubscribe, HELP for help. See the
> [Privacy Policy](./privacy-policy.md) and [SMS / Messaging Terms](./sms-messaging-terms.md).

The first message you receive is a branded welcome confirming your enrollment and
repeating the STOP/HELP instructions.

## Message frequency

**Message frequency varies** based on activity on your project — from a few messages per
week to occasional messages.

## Message and data rates

**Message and data rates may apply**, depending on your mobile carrier and plan. Siapp
does not charge recipients for messages.

## Opt-out and help

- Reply **STOP** at any time to unsubscribe. You will receive a confirmation and no
  further messages unless you opt in again.
- Reply **HELP** for assistance, or contact support@siapp.app / +1 (206) 596-7128.
- We process opt-outs across all senders and honor them going forward.

## Privacy

We do not share, sell, or rent mobile numbers or messaging consent to third parties or
affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile numbers are shared
only with the providers needed to deliver messages you opted in to receive (e.g. Twilio,
Meta). See our [Privacy Policy](./privacy-policy.md).
